FixTech v6.22 - Browser Tab Icon Fix

1. Extract index.html directly into your current FixTech-Hosting folder.
2. Replace the existing index.html.
3. Run OPEN-ADMIN-v6.21.bat (or your current localhost launcher).
4. Settings -> Publishing -> Prepare Website for GitHub.
5. Extract the new FixTech-GitHub-Upload.zip and upload/replace its contents in GitHub.
6. Hard-refresh the live website (Ctrl+F5).

The FixTech tab icon is embedded directly in index.html, so it works on Home and all clean route folders without relying on a relative favicon path.
